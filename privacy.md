# CK-DOS Privacy Policy

_Effective date: October 13, 2026_

This policy describes how **CK-DOS** handles data. The developer and support
contact is **Christian Klukas**,
[christian.klukas@gmail.com](mailto:christian.klukas@gmail.com).

## Data on your Mac

CK-DOS runs your selected programs and processes your files on your Mac.
Profiles, file-access bookmarks, saved-session information, trial timing
information and performance reports are stored locally. Saved sessions and
exported output are written to destinations you choose. The app does not
automatically send these files or reports to the developer or a
CK-DOS-operated server.

The app requires no CK-DOS account. It does not use advertising or tracking
SDKs or automatically upload developer analytics or crash reports.

## Performance checks

The performance check runs first-party test workloads locally. Its report
includes information about the Mac and the current environment, such as the
model, macOS version, processors, memory, power and thermal state,
audio/display environment, and app, workload and asset identities.

This information helps assess results and identify outdated reports. Reports
are cached locally. If you export and share one, review it before sending.
CK-DOS does not automatically upload performance reports.

## Purchases and the free trial

In-app purchases and the free trial use Apple's StoreKit and App Store
services. Apple handles payment and account authentication. CK-DOS checks
verified transactions to enable purchased features and determine the trial
period. It does not operate its own purchase-account or receipt-validation
server, and the developer does not receive your payment-card details from
the app.

Apple's handling of App Store transactions is governed by
[Apple's App Store privacy information](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/)
and your Apple account settings.

## Optional guest networking

The virtual network card is disabled in a new profile. When you enable
outbound networking, guest applications can send information to their chosen
destinations through your Mac. DNS queries use the Mac's resolver. Destinations
and network or DNS providers have their own privacy practices.

If you enable the web compatibility proxy, CK-DOS processes guest web traffic
locally and makes HTTPS connections to selected sites. The proxy can read
requests and responses while servicing them. It does not send that traffic to
a CK-DOS-operated service. Leaving the guest network disabled prevents this
guest-network traffic.

## Files, printing and connected devices

CK-DOS accesses the files and folders you select for your workflow. Saved
file-access bookmarks allow the app to reuse an authorized selection later.
Guest programs can change writable files or disk images that you make
available to them.

Printing and physical COM-port use pass information to the printer or
connected device you choose. Those devices, services and their providers can
have their own data-handling practices.

## Support messages and this website

If you email support, the developer receives the message, your sender details
and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond
to your request and investigate the reported issue. Review attachments and
remove private information before sending. You can email the contact above
with questions about a support message or to request its deletion.

Support email is handled through the email provider. This website is hosted
on GitHub Pages, so requests to it are also subject to
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
The site does not add analytics, advertising scripts or a customer sign-in
service.

## Your choices

You can choose which files and devices to grant access to, leave guest
networking disabled, decline to share diagnostic files, and continue using
Free without activating a trial or making a purchase.

Saved documents and exported output can be removed from the locations where
you saved them. Uninstalling an app does not necessarily remove every saved
document or preference on macOS. Contact support if you need help identifying
CK-DOS data before deleting it.
